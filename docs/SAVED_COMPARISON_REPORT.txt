The completed blind Pantheon round was reused; no Pantheon session or statistical
model was rerun for this comparison. Its data, QC, inclusion and method choices
preceded access to your old workflow/results. It was evaluator-assisted: software
dependencies were provisioned and its pre-DE recommendation to exclude wu16 was
accepted. Its original plan, amendments, conversations and execution logs remain
saved. All 92 original project files still match their baseline hashes, sizes
and modification times.

The principal gene-level expression patterns agree. Across 6,601 genes tested
by both, log2 fold changes correlate at r=0.988 (Spearman=0.980), with a median
absolute difference of 0.067 log2 units. All 1,282 commonly tested genes
significant in either analysis agree in direction. The 422 direction reversals
are small, nonsignificant effects (absolute log2FC below 0.43 in both).

For example, both find higher S100P (+7.97/+7.83 original/Pantheon), MIA
(+8.80/+9.10) and MUC5AC (+5.95/+5.78), and lower NR5A2 (-2.89/-2.95), FOLR1
(-4.72/-5.01) and CRISP3 (-6.50/-6.80). No prior prose biological conclusion
was assumed: this assessment uses the actual result tables. These are measured
expression differences, not evidence that the genes cause malignancy.

Different filtering explains many unique discoveries. Pantheon-only CYP2S1,
PLAC8, IL2RG and TNS4 each met the original count threshold in only seven of
16 profiles; the original required eight. They pass Pantheon's seven-patient
CPM filter. Conversely, original-only SERPINA6, HABP2, ANPEP and TPM2 fail
Pantheon's filter. Missing tests are not negative findings. Among genes tested
by both, GABRP remains higher (+3.19 versus +2.65), but adjusted p changes from
0.000141 to 0.0845. This is weaker statistical evidence, not reversed biology.

The original used paired PyDESeq2 0.4.12; Pantheon independently chose paired,
robust edgeR 4.4.0 quasi-likelihood testing with TMM normalization and
filterByExpr. With the SAME eight patients and exactly identical raw
pseudobulks, saved edgeR results yield 912 discoveries, 888 shared with the
original. Common-gene effect correlation is 0.9984; median difference is 0.0149.
Thus normalization, filtering and inference choices explain substantial list
differences even before the wu16 exclusion. Neither discovery count establishes
which analysis has better biological accuracy.

Pantheon excluded wu16 before DE because HBB accounts for 80.3% of its malignant
pseudobulk counts; 103/127 labelled malignant cells have over 50% HBB counts.
This could reflect contamination or label/composition problems; its cause is
unresolved. Within edgeR, exclusion changes 912 discoveries to 1,007: 809 are
shared, 198 gained (39 previously filtered, 159 nonsignificant), and 103 lost
(9 now filtered, 94 nonsignificant). Saved fixed-gene-mask leave-one-out effects
closely match the seven-patient primary, supporting an effect of donor removal
on estimates beyond filter membership. These diagnostics have no new p-values.
GABARAP illustrates the change: edgeR8 -0.75/FDR 0.164 versus primary7
-1.04/FDR 0.00708. Exclusion also refits normalization and dispersion; this is
not a clean attribution to one statistical component.

Other sample choices matter. Saved minimum-five-cell and minimum-20-cell
sensitivities find 879 and 537 genes, respectively; both retain wu16 and are
not isolated threshold comparisons with the primary. Minimum five adds wu07
and basal-rich wu39. Removing wu39 from that cohort changes 879 to 949
discoveries (801 shared); removing wu07 gives 851 (756 shared). These variations
limit a claim of one definitive gene list. The primary malignant cells are
1,837 classical-labelled and only 19 basal-labelled, so the conclusions mainly
apply to classical-labelled epithelium.

Both workflows already reproduced computationally in saved checks, and this
comparison confirms exact eight-patient count equality and exact seven-patient
subsetting. No barcode, pairing or count-aggregation error was found in the
original. Its >=200-gene / <=25%-mitochondrial QC removed no epithelial cells
and missed the wu16 composition issue. Its dispersion-trend fallback to a mean
fit is reproducible and should remain documented. Pantheon's extreme HOXB6
estimate also deserves caution because comparator counts are zero and adjusted
residual degrees of freedom approach zero.

"Normal Epithelial" denotes putatively nonmalignant cells annotated within
tumor biopsies, not healthy-donor controls. Labels partly use expression and
inferred CNA, so some marker contrasts may reflect the labelling procedure.
The seven-patient primary and sensitivity analyses do not establish causality,
a general basal signature, or independent biological replication. Study context:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41698-023-00455-z
